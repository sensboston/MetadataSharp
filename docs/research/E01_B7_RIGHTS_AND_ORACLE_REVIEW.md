# E01 B7 — per-format rights and oracle review (in progress)

B7 is a read-only census/probe batch, not authorization for a production
format API or write support. The developer authorized continuation without
another confirmation, but the approved census design still requires a separate
rights review for each format before authoring its fixtures. This ledger permits
narrow independently authored synthetic probes below; no imported sample is cleared.
The initial table below records review history, not an observation, refusal or
exclusion. B7 observations are in `evidence/e01-b7-census/`; eight CR3 rows
remain rights-blocked and other metadata/decoder gaps remain open.

## Source and sample policy

- Use independent authored bytes or item-level verified CC0/public-domain
  samples only. Record recipe, origin, terms, hashes, limits, and independent
  expected facts. Do not copy Exiv2 tests, issue attachments, camera files,
  source code, or unrelated data. A repository-level license is not proof of
  rights for every image, specification, patent, or sample.
- Pinned Exiv2 0.28.9 is the external black-box baseline; ExifTool 12.76 is
  the secondary differential reference. The Git tag `12.76` resolves to
  `212f02cf22b9484674752181cad3b73089ce9694`; a prior local external checkout
  reported version 12.76. It was not vendored and is not present in every
  workspace. The observer and CI now verify the pinned checkout and record invocations;
  availability alone is not an observation.
- Neither tool's source, code, tag dictionary, tests, or outputs are a
  normative oracle or a production dependency. Recognition or metadata read
  does not prove an image decodes. BMFF item extents and relationships require
  bounded structural checks; no independent raw/coded-image decoder has yet
  been established as an oracle.

## Narrow synthetic-fixture rights decisions

Separate decisions below cover only independently authored, tiny image pixels
encoded locally for black-box research. No upstream sample, specification text,
code, test, dictionary, camera capture, or owner metadata is imported. The
encoder and its dependencies are external research tools, not runtime
libraries. Copyright provenance of generated bytes does not resolve codec patent
or product-distribution questions; no production format or publication is
approved. Normative conformance and independent decoder gaps remain explicit.

- **AVIF:** permit a small AV1-encoded authored image generated by pinned Pillow
  and its bundled AVIF encoder, with manifest/recipe/hashes. AOM's pinned spec
  overview and brand/box sections were reviewed. A brand alone is not a valid
  image; require successful decode and independent structural observations.
- **HEIF:** permit authored synthetic images encoded through pinned pillow-heif
  and its bundled libheif/x265, with exact versions and provenance. The
  [Nokia HEIF overview](https://github.com/nokiatech/heif/blob/363cf942bd45ec6772c52b04d7d0b016801c2db4/README.md)
  describes an ISO-BMFF container and explicitly warns that codec patents are
  not granted. This is an overview, not a license to copy its examples or a
  substitute for normative conformance. No vendor samples are used.
- **HEIC:** reviewed separately as the HEVC-coded HEIF case. Permit only
  independently encoded pixels from our recipe and label the codec and brand;
  never equate a filename or generic HEIF recognition with verified HEIC
  semantics. The same patent and normative limitations apply.
- **JXL:** permit synthetic authored pixels encoded by pinned imagecodecs/libjxl
  in both naked codestream and container forms. The pinned libjxl format overview
  identifies these forms separately. Its files/samples and implementation are
  not imported; same-library decode is not an independent decoder.
- **CR3:** blocked for the present batch: no independently reviewed Canon RAW
  layout or owned/reliably licensed camera image has been established. Do not
  create a counterfeit container and claim a CR3 observation; retain all cells
  as blocked/pending, not excluded. This is an evidence gap, not a product refusal.

## AVIF source review progress

The pinned AOM source's `brands-overview`, `image-and-image-collection-brand`,
`avif-boxes` and `avif-required-boxes` sections were inspected without copying
its fixtures or implementation. They distinguish brand identification from
actual compliance, identify AV1 primary-image relationships, and give a required
box hierarchy based on ISOBMFF/HEIF/MIAF. A bare `ftyp`/brand is therefore not
enough to claim a valid AVIF. Underlying normative editions, sample rights,
AV1-coded item provenance and independent decode remain unresolved: this review
does **not** establish AVIF conformance or assert supported decoding.

`tools/reference/bmff_extents.py` accounts for caller-supplied byte ranges and
reports duplicates/overlap with finite limits. It does not parse `iloc` or infer
how a construction method resolves; such claims need reviewed normative sources.
Its tests use generic byte strings, not format fixtures.

The Exiv2 operation seed ledger records read claims for EXIF, IPTC, XMP, and
thumbnail, and no documented claim for comments and ICC, for each of these
five formats. A no-claim cell remains in scope and must be attempted or honestly
blocked, not converted to a claim of absence. The generic `bmff_structure.py`
helper checks flat box bounds, `ftyp` structure, and JXL signatures only; toy
unit-test bytes are not format fixtures and cannot prove conformance or support.
Before B7 is marked complete, record per-format rights decisions, fixture
provenance, pinned CLI invocations and build options, secondary-oracle results
or explicit gaps, and honest statuses. Denominators remain unfrozen and
MetadataSharp verification remains not-assessed.
